Step 3. Opinion words: good 👍 or bad 👎?

These words are not genres. They say what you think about a plot, a character or an ending.

WordEasy EnglishПо-русски
grippingso interesting that you can’t stop readingзахватывающий
mysteriousstrange, full of secretsзагадочный
poorly-developedbadly made, not enough detailплохо проработанный
well-roundedlike a real person: has good and bad sides, feelings, a pastмногогранный, проработанный
likeablenice; easy to likeсимпатичный, приятный
shallownot deep, not serious; we know very little about themповерхностный

Step 4. Which words go together?

In English some words are “friends” (collocations). We say a gripping plot, a likeable character, an unexpected ending.

Careful! classic ≠ classical
classic = famous and very good for many years: a classic story, a classic novel ✔
classical = about traditional music or Ancient Greece / Rome: classical music ✔   a classical story ✘
